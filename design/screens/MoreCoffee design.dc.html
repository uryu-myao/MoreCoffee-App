Turn 5 · Recording a cup — the 15-second path
Where Record a coffee goes. The brief's 15 seconds only works if the fast path asks for the one thing the app cannot know — how it tasted to you — and pre-fills everything else from My gear and My beans. So 5a asks in the order a person actually answers: rating, then Who made it?, which swaps the block under it — I made it → From my shelf, Barista → Café details, Ready-to-drink → Product — each pre-filled from your own history, with the height animating between modes (the chips are live; tap them). Everything but the photo is required, marked by a single brown square and confirmed with a quiet check. Save stays live throughout; 5d is what a tap on Save looks like when something is genuinely empty. 5e is the finished Ready-to-drink state as its own screen: brewing and café disappear entirely, Product carries brand and package, and the only extra is an optional store-and-price line — two required answers instead of three. The tasting note in 5b is opt-in and is where taste DNA gets fed; 5c closes the loop.
